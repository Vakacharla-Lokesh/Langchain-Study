**1. Fine dining (5★)** "We celebrated our anniversary at The Oberoi's restaurant, and it was flawless from start to finish. The tasting menu was a journey — the lamb rogan josh was smoky and deeply spiced, and the saffron rice pudding for dessert was pure comfort. Our server checked in just often enough without hovering, and the live sitar in the corner set a mood that made the evening feel special. Expensive, yes, but worth it for a once-in-a-while night."
**2. Street food stall (4★)** "Stopped by this chaat corner in Chandni Chowk on a Sunday evening. The pani puri was crisp, the chole bhature was piping hot, and the chutneys were tangy in the best way. The only issue is the queue — we waited 20 minutes for four people. Cash only, no seating, and the plates are single-use. But for the taste-to-price ratio, it's unbeatable."
**3. Casual family restaurant (4★)** "Take the whole family here on a Saturday and you'll be happy. The butter chicken is rich without being heavy, and the garlic naan is the best I've had outside of Delhi. Service is fast even when the place is packed — our server refilled water without being asked. The AC is a bit weak in summer, and parking is a nightmare, but the food keeps us coming back."
**4. Rooftop bar-restaurant (3★)** "The view is genuinely stunning at sunset, and the cocktails are well-crafted. But the food is where it falls short — the grilled chicken was dry and overpriced for the portion size. Service was friendly but slow; we waited 35 minutes for our mains. Go for the vibe and a drink, but don't expect a full meal experience."
**5. South Indian restaurant (5★)** "The idli was soft and steamed to perfection, and the sambar had that deep, slow-cooked flavor you only get when someone actually takes time with the dal. The filter coffee was strong and frothy, exactly how it should be. The staff was warm and even recommended a dish I'd never tried — the mysore pak was a revelation. Clean, affordable, and the kind of place that feels like a local's favorite."
**6. Italian bistro (4★)** "The truffle pasta was rich and the mushroom risotto was creamy without being gloopy. The bread basket came out warm and the olive oil was clearly good quality. Our server was knowledgeable about the wine list and paired a crisp Soave with the seafood platter perfectly. The room gets a little noisy on weekends, and the check came a bit late, but the food is consistently excellent."
**7. Fast-casual burger joint (3★)** "The smash burger is decent — the patty is juicy and the bun is soft. But the fries were lukewarm and the ketchup packets were running out by the time we finished. Service was quick in and out, which is what you want here. It's fine for a quick lunch, but I wouldn't plan a sit-down meal. The seating is tight and the music is a bit loud."
**8. Regional thali place (5★)** "Forty items on the plate, and almost every single one was good. The dal makhani was creamy, the aloo gobhi was properly spiced, and the papad was crisp. The raita cut through the richness perfectly. Service was a bit rushed — they refilled the rice without asking — but at this price point, that's the only complaint. It's the kind of place you go when you want a full, honest meal without pretension."
**9. Seafood restaurant (4★)** "The prawn butter was generous and the fish was clearly fresh — you can tell when a place sources daily. The tandoori squid was smoky and tender. The only letdown was the garlic bread, which was bland and a little stale. The waiter was attentive and the table spacing is comfortable, so you don't feel crammed. Solid choice if you're in the mood for something coastal."
**10. Late-night dhaba (4★)** "Hit this dhaba at 1 AM after a road trip, and the dal makhani and tandoori roti were exactly what we needed. The dal was slow-cooked and the naan was charred just right. No menu, no frills, and the service is just "you order, we bring it." The chai was strong and the parking lot was full, which tells you everything. Not a place for a date, but for a group of friends after a long drive, it's perfect."
